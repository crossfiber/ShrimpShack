What if your team was always on, no matter where you are from?

Most Keys bars carry the local feed and a couple of national games. The Shack runs the full streaming slate on every screen, so a Bills fan from Buffalo gets his game at the same time the Dolphins crowd gets theirs. Say the team when you sit down. It goes on.

Every game, every window, from the 1:00 kickoffs through the late game. Sunday Night Football on the big screens.

Everything else

  • Do you really show every game?

    Yes. The Shack carries the full streaming slate, so every NFL game on Sunday, the Monday and Thursday night games, and college Saturdays are all on. Tell the bartender your team and it goes on a screen.

  • Can I bring in my own catch?

    Yes. Hook n' Cook is the house program: bring your cleaned catch and the kitchen prepares it Continental, Senator, Piccata, grilled, blackened, Cajun fried or coconut fried, with sides.

  • Do I need a reservation on game day?

    Walk-ins are welcome every day. For a group or a big game, call (305) 664-8433 and the room is held for you.

Seventy-nine years on this corner

The building went up before the highway had its current name. Every owner since has cooked fish in it.

1947

The building goes up

A single-story block building on the oceanside of the highway, 100 feet of frontage, 4,942 square feet. It has never been anything but a place to eat.

Monroe County property record
1992

The Continental Style is born here

At Squid Row, the restaurant that held this address before the Shack, a cook panko-crusts a fillet and finishes it with key lime butter, tomato, scallion and Parmesan. It has not left the menu since.

Still printed on the menu: "Created right here at Squid Row in 1992"
2011

Squid Row becomes the Shrimp Shack

December 2011. A mother and daughter team, Jill and Morgan, open the Islamorada Shrimp Shack as a neighborhood spot, not a tourist trap. The servers get a name of their own: the Shrimpettes.

2015

Guy Fieri pulls up

December 2015. Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives films "Smoke and Seafood" in the kitchen. The fritters, the Fish Senator and the Shrimp N' Grits all make Guy's Picks. The episode airs March 11, 2016.

Food Network, Season 24, Episode 4
2026

The Epsteins take the keys

Brian and David Epstein, sons of Miami Herald fishing editor Bob Epstein and owners of the Poppa Wahoo Collective, buy the Shack. Veteran owned, family run, and the kitchen keeps every recipe. [MONTH OF PURCHASE NEEDED]
