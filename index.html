Our story

Seventy-nine years of cooking fish on this corner

  • The building went up in 1947 and has never been anything but a place to eat.
  • The Continental Style was invented in this kitchen in 1992, back when it was Squid Row.
  • Guy Fieri filmed here in 2015 and gave three dishes a Guy's Pick.
  • Today it belongs to the Epstein family of the Poppa Wahoo charter fleet. Veteran owned.

Events and catering

Paint nights, private parties, catering from backyard to black tie

The back room seats a full party under the beams, and the kitchen travels: shrimp skewers, ceviche, surf and turf platters, whole key lime pies. Twenty-four hours notice, no minimum order.
